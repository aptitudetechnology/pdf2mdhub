Welcome to PDF2MD Hub!

Your solution for converting PDFs to Markdown and managing your documents.

Upload New PDF View All Documents WebContainer Demo

How it Works:

  1. Upload a PDF: Simply drag and drop your PDF file or select it from your computer.
  2. Automatic Conversion: The system converts your PDF to Markdown in the background.
  3. View & Manage: See the converted content, download the Markdown, add notes, and manage document details.

About This Project:

This application demonstrates server-side PDF to Markdown conversion using opendocsg/pdf2md and Flask.

It's designed to be a starting point for document management and content extraction.
